### Socratic exchange

**Q:** You showed v + (−1)v = 0. What lets you go from "an additive inverse" to "(−1)v = −v specifically"?

**A (initial):** Claimed uniqueness of additive inverse is axiomatic.

**Correction noted:** Uniqueness is NOT an axiom — Definition 1.19 only asserts existence. Uniqueness is Theorem 1.26 (derived from associativity + commutativity). The full argument needs to cite that theorem. Distinction between axiom and derived fact flagged as important for Axler's style throughout.

### Faded examples and you-try

- **(−1)v = −v:** v + (−1)v = 1v + (−1)v = (1+(−1))v = 0v = 0, so (−1)v is an additive inverse of v. ✓ (needed Thm 1.26 for uniqueness step)
