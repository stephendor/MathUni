### Socratic exchange

**Q:** In the √2 proof, why does "p even and q even" produce a contradiction — and could you skip the "lowest terms" assumption?

**A:** No — "lowest terms" is the bomb itself. Assuming gcd(p,q)=1 is what gets contradicted when both turn out to be even (divisible by 2). Without it, two even numbers aren't a contradiction; you'd need infinite descent instead, which is a different and messier argument.
