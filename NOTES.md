# Learner Notes

Preferences and teaching adjustments. Updated by any session that learns
something about how Stephen learns. Newest entries at top, dated.

## Abstract Algebra module (2026-07-22, supersedes the morning's gt note)
- The Semester-1 group-theory course was replaced by one continuous **Abstract
  Algebra** module (`aa`) following Aluffi *Notes from the Underground* in its
  native order — rings, modules, abelian groups, groups (ch. 1-12). Rationale: a
  group-first course fights Aluffi's design, and modules + the PID structure
  theorem are the honest foundation for persistent homology (persistence modules,
  barcodes). Full arc incl. Sylow/solvable; fields/Galois out of scope.
- Carter/Macauley are retained as the **visual layer for the groups units only**
  (aa-00, aa-23, aa-25); this refines (does not contradict) the standing
  "loves visual-first for groups" note below. Rings/modules units are Aluffi-native.
- The studied gt-01 became **aa-00**, an optional prereq-free "why algebra?"
  motivation aside; gt-02 was retired (its group-axioms content is now aa-23).

## Confirmed effective (2026-07-07)
- Quickfire warm-up starts and embedded reinforcement self-checks in lessons
  confirmed by Stephen as strong for focus and retention — keep both in all
  new lessons and reviews.

## Standing preferences (2026-07-03)
- Loves: Aluffi's expository style, Stillwell's geometric-historical
  approach, visual-first treatments (Macauley/Carter for groups).
- Lessons open with the hook, never with "Definition 1.1".
- Proof feedback: rigorous and direct; hand-waves named as hand-waves.
- Timebox segments to 25–30 min with explicit breaks.
- Prefers markdown-converted texts (Core Texts `md\` folder) for quoting;
  PDFs are fallback and page-citation source.
