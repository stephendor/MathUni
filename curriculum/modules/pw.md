# Module: Proof Workshop (pw) — Semester 1

**Mission link:** Every TDA paper you'll verify is built from these moves —
direct proof, induction, quantifier discipline, epsilon-delta craft. The
capstone is written mathematics; proof style is substance, not decoration.

**Primary text:** Cummings, *Proofs* — Core Texts folder (prefer `md\`, cite
PDF pages).
**Support:** Schumacher, *Chapter Zero*; Earl, *Towards Higher Mathematics*.

**Units (in DAG order):** pw-01 … pw-05 (see syllabus.yaml; pw-01 is the
root unlocking pw-02/pw-03, which feed pw-04 (with an-03) and pw-05).

**Format:** Proof Workshop is woven, not sequential — it unlocks early and
runs alongside la/an/gt for the whole semester. Beyond the five core units,
the module runs as a **weekly forensic proof critique**: one deliberately
flawed or subtle proof per week, rotating its source across the live
modules (an argument from Axler one week, from Aluffi the next, from Abbott
the week after). The learner finds and names every planted or genuine flaw
before seeing the diagnosis.

**Teaching notes:**
- Keep pw-01/pw-02/pw-03 ahead of where la/an/gt need them; pw-04 must land
  before an's epsilon-delta content (an-10) is reached.
- The weekly critique is the module's real engine after pw-05 — treat it as
  a standing lesson slot, not an occasional extra.
- Feedback is rigorous and direct: name hand-waves as hand-waves, per
  learner preference.

**Assessment:**
- Unit mastery quizzes (SRS + 3-5 questions) for pw-01 through pw-05.
- No module problem set. Instead: the weekly forensic critique is the
  ongoing assessment instrument — each critique is scored on flaws found vs
  flaws planted, tracked as a rolling record rather than a single gated test.

**Common misconceptions to watch** (seed for learning-records):
- Proof by example (treating a verified instance as a general proof).
- Circularity (assuming the conclusion, or an equivalent restatement of it,
  as a step).
- Quantifier order (swapping "for all... there exists" with "there exists...
  for all", especially under negation).
